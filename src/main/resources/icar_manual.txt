DISEASE: Aphids
TECHNICAL REMEDY: Apply synthetic Imidacloprid at 0.5ml per liter of water. Ensure full foliage coverage.
ORGANIC VEDIC ALTERNATIVE: Prepare Neem-based spray. Crush 5kg of Neem leaves, soak in water for 24 hours, boil, and mix with 100L of water. Add 200g of soap flakes as a sticker.

DISEASE: Powdery Mildew
TECHNICAL REMEDY: Spray chemical fungicide Hexaconazole 5% SC at 2ml per liter of water.
ORGANIC VEDIC ALTERNATIVE: Use Sour Buttermilk spray. Take 5 liters of 3-day old sour buttermilk, mix with 100 liters of water, and spray across the affected crop field.

DISEASE: Nitrogen Deficiency
TECHNICAL REMEDY: Apply 50kg of synthetic Urea per acre during the vegetative growth phase.
ORGANIC VEDIC ALTERNATIVE: Prepare Jivamrut. Mix 10kg cow dung, 10L cow urine, 1kg jaggery, 1kg gram flour, and a handful of undisturbed soil. Ferment for 48 hours and apply to the soil.
